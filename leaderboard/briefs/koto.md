# Koto

Koto is a Tokyo studio making minimalist mechanical keyboards.

## Colours
- Primary `#111111` for actions and key highlights.
- Secondary `#e8e4dc` for supporting surfaces.
- Accent `#ff4f1f`, sparingly.
- Background `#fafaf7`, text `#111111`.

## Typography
- Headings: Space Grotesk. Body: IBM Plex Sans. Load both from Google Fonts.

## Shape and space
- Corner radii: 0px, 2px only.
- Spacing on a 4px grid.

## Voice
Precise and terse. Short sentences, specs over adjectives.
