# Fernwood

Fernwood is a mail-order nursery for rare native plants.

## Colours
- Primary `#2f5d3a` for actions and key highlights.
- Secondary `#c8a96b` for supporting surfaces.
- Accent `#e2683c`, sparingly.
- Background `#f6f1e7`, text `#1f2a1f`.

## Typography
- Headings: Fraunces. Body: Work Sans. Load both from Google Fonts.

## Shape and space
- Corner radii: 4px, 10px only.
- Spacing on a 8px grid.

## Voice
Warm, plain-spoken, a little botanical. Never shouts.
