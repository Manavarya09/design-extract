# Halcyon

Halcyon is a sleep and recovery clinic.

## Colours
- Primary `#3a4a8c` for actions and key highlights.
- Secondary `#a9c4e8` for supporting surfaces.
- Accent `#f2c46d`, sparingly.
- Background `#f4f6fb`, text `#1b2340`.

## Typography
- Headings: DM Serif Display. Body: Nunito Sans. Load both from Google Fonts.

## Shape and space
- Corner radii: 12px, 24px only.
- Spacing on a 8px grid.

## Voice
Calm, reassuring, clinical where it matters.
